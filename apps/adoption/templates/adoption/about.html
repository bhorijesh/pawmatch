About PawMatch

Connecting loving homes with pets in need — powered by smart algorithms.

📐

Haversine Distance

We calculate the great-circle distance between you and each shelter using the Haversine formula, so you always see the nearest adoptable pets first.

🎯

Cosine Similarity

Your adoption history is encoded as feature vectors (species, size, age, temperament). Cosine similarity finds pets that match your preferences.

Our Mission

Every pet deserves a forever home. PawMatch makes adoption simple, transparent, and personalized for both adopters and shelters.
