About PawMatch

We help loving families find the right pet — and help shelters place every animal in a forever home.

PawMatch is a pet adoption platform built around one simple idea: the right match matters. Whether you are looking for a playful puppy, a calm senior cat, or a small companion for your apartment, we make it easy to discover adoptable pets near you, learn about their personality, and start the adoption process — all in one place.

What Makes PawMatch Different

Thoughtful tools that put pets and people first

📍

Pets Near You

Share your location and browse adoptable pets sorted by distance. Our map view shows shelters and available animals in your area so you can visit the ones closest to home.

✨

Matches That Fit You

The more you explore and apply, the better PawMatch understands what you are looking for — species, size, age, and temperament — and surfaces pets that fit your lifestyle.

🏠

Simple Adoption Flow

Read detailed pet profiles, submit an application online, and track your status from one dashboard. No phone tag, no guesswork — just a clear path from browsing to bringing your pet home.

🤝

Built for Shelters Too

Shelter staff get a dedicated dashboard to list pets, manage applications, and connect with adopters. PawMatch supports the people doing the hard work on the ground.

How It Works

From first search to forever home in four steps

1

Browse & Search

Explore our catalog of adoptable pets. Filter by species, size, and more — or use your location to see who is waiting nearby.

2

Find Your Match

Read each pet's profile — photos, traits, temperament, and adoption fee — and save the ones that feel like a good fit.

3

Apply Online

Submit an adoption application directly through PawMatch. Shelters review your request and reach out when it is time to meet.

4

Welcome Home

Complete the adoption with your shelter, and give a deserving animal the loving home they have been waiting for.

Who PawMatch Is For

Supporting both sides of every adoption

For Adopters

  • Distance-sorted results and an interactive map
